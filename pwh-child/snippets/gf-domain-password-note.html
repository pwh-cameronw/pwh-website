Domain password

Complete this section only if you’re transferring your domain to Perth Web Hosting. We need your domain password (also called an authorisation code) to complete the transfer.

For a .au domain, request the password through auDA’s domain password recovery service. For other domains, check your current registrar’s online account or contact their support team.

The registrant email address must be one you can access. If it isn’t, update it through your current registrar before requesting the transfer. We can’t transfer the domain until you can access the registrant email account.
