Domain password

Only fill this in if you're transferring your domain name to us.

To transfer your domain to Perth Web Hosting, we need its domain password. If you don't have it:

  • .au domains: request the password through auDA's domain password recovery. It's sent to the domain's registrant email address, so that address needs to be one you can access. If it isn't, you can usually update it by logging in to your current registrar's member portal.
  • Other domains: log in to your current registrar's member portal, or submit a support ticket to your current registrar.

We can't transfer a domain name until its registrant email address is one you can access.
